About

I am a glaciologist and remote sensing researcher. I completed my doctoral research in Geospatial Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, where my dissertation investigated high-spatiotemporal glacier change and driving mechanisms in the western Himalaya through terrestrial laser scanning, time-lapse photogrammetry, UAV surveys, and satellite remote sensing.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, monitoring rock glaciers in the Trans-Himalaya using SAR-based satellite imagery and ground-based GPS sensors to reveal where instability is growing.

My research spans ice-cliff backwasting dynamics, debris-covered glacier behaviour, and glacier surface velocity estimation. Alongside the primary glacier research, I have worked on SAR-based terrain classification as part of the NISAR mission testbed, UAV and LiDAR surveys for heritage documentation, and large-scale dumpsite volumetric estimation using drone-derived DSMs. I completed my Master of Technology in Geo-Informatics with a Gold Medal from MNNIT Allahabad. I am an active member of the International Glaciological Society, the American Geophysical Union, and the European Geosciences Union.

Projects
Post Doctoral FellowAugust 2026 – Present
Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, India

Doctoral Research: High-Spatiotemporal Glacier Change, Western HimalayaJuly 2021 – June 2026
IIT Roorkee · Supervisor: Prof. Saurabh Vijay

Using terrestrial laser scanners deployed repeatedly over multiple seasons, high-resolution point clouds capture glacier surface evolution at centimetre precision. Time-lapse cameras track ice-cliff retreat and surface displacement. The work quantifies backwasting rates at Machoi Glacier and ice-flow velocities at Drang Drung Glacier with sub-seasonal resolution.

SAR-Based Glacier Terrain Classification for the NISAR Mission2022 – 2024
IIT Roorkee · Collaborative

Evaluated quad-polarimetric dual-frequency airborne SAR to map glacierised terrain as preparatory science for NASA/ISRO NISAR. Applied decomposition techniques and ML classifiers to distinguish glacier facies across L- and S-band datasets.

Endangered Wood Architecture Preservation (EWAP)Feb – Mar 2026
IIT Roorkee · PI: Prof. Ram Sateesh Pasupuleti and Prof. Saurabh Vijay

UAV photogrammetry, TLS, and close-range photography produced centimetre-accurate 3D models of two centuries-old wooden temples in Uttarkashi. Complemented by ethnographic fieldwork with local communities.

Heritage Impact Assessment for UNESCO World Heritage SitesJun – Oct 2024
Punjab and Haryana High Court, Chandigarh

UAV surveys and 3D modelling around Le Corbusier's Capitol Complex. Visibility and line-of-sight analyses informed the Court's heritage conservation decisions.

Dumpsite Garbage Mass Estimation Using UAV PhotogrammetryOct 2022 – Sep 2024
Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Roorkee

Fourteen dumpsite surveys across UP, Haryana, and Uttarakhand. Drone photogrammetry combined with depth-wise bulk density sampling to estimate garbage mass more accurately than conventional methods.
